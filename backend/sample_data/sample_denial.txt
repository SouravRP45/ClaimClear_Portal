REASON FOR DENIAL:

The requested service — Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) of the lumbar spine (CPT code 72148) — has been denied as not medically necessary pursuant to Section 4.2 (Medical Necessity Criteria) of your Meridian Health Insurance Plan Document (Group Plan No. MHI-GRP-2019-0044).

Our clinical review team, utilizing Meridian's proprietary evidence-based clinical criteria, determined that the medical records submitted do not demonstrate that you have completed a required minimum course of conservative treatment (defined as at least six [6] weeks of documented physical therapy and/or chiropractic care) prior to the MRI being ordered. Under Section 4.2, diagnostic imaging for musculoskeletal conditions requires documented failure of conservative treatment before advanced imaging will be considered a covered benefit.

The treating physician's referral documentation does not include records confirming that conservative care was pursued and found ineffective. In the absence of this documentation, the clinical criteria for advanced diagnostic imaging have not been met under the terms of your current plan.

THIS IS NOT A COVERAGE DENIAL. Your plan does provide coverage for MRI procedures; however, coverage in this instance is conditioned upon satisfaction of the medical necessity criteria outlined in Section 4.2 of your plan document.
